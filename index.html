What is Due Diligence?

Due Diligence is an independent market research project. Every trading idea is written down before it is tested, tested on market data it has never seen, and reported plainly, including when it does not work.

It is coded with AI assistance and built to an academic standard: sources are cited in APA style, and the numbers on the scanner are checked automatically against their sources.

Research and education only. Nothing here is investment advice, and trading can lose real money.
