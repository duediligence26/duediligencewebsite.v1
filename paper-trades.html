PAPER Not real money · After the close

MapleStax CBC paper trades.

The desk is paper-trading one strategy: the MapleStax CBC signal, a TradingView indicator that flips long or short on the 10-minute chart. Each signal is a same-day (0DTE) option. The paper book trades SPY. QQQ is added starting November 6, 2026. Two versions run side by side on the same signal. These are simulated trades. They are not live orders and not real money.

The fill

Both versions are entered at the ask and exited at the bid. A paper trade stays off this page until it is closed. The record is posted after the close. One signal can show up twice, once in each version.

1 contract

One contract. A stop closes the paper trade at −30%. A profit lock protects an open gain. A target closes it at +100%. An opposite signal flips the paper trade out.

4 contract scale-out

Four contracts. Sell one at +40%, one at +60%, and one at +100%. The last contract trails. After the first sale, the stop moves to breakeven. Anything still open is out by 3:40 p.m. Eastern. In the table, the exit is the average sale price and P&L is the combined dollars.
